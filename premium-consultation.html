Before you book

What a consultation will not do

Most of this profession is sold on certainty. I would rather you arrive with an accurate expectation, because a reading is only useful to someone who knows what they are receiving.

  • It will not name a date for an event. Dasha and transit describe periods and their character. They do not guarantee outcomes within them.
  • It will not overrule medical or legal advice. Where a question is properly clinical or legal, I will say so and decline to substitute for a professional.
  • It will not be reliable at the house level without a reliable birth time. The ascendant shifts a full degree every four minutes. Where your time is uncertain, the reading leans on the Moon chart and dasha instead.
  • It will not remove a difficult period. Remedies moderate how a period is met. They do not delete it.
  • It will not tell you what to do. The chart describes terrain. The decision remains yours.

Questions

Asked before every first session

Is the reading done by a human or generated by software?

Every consultation is conducted live, one-to-one, over video by Shashank Agrawal. The chart is cast with astronomical software — as it is by every practising astrologer — but the analysis, the written report and the conversation are entirely human. No part of the reading is auto-generated.

Will I be sold gemstones or remedies afterwards?

No. There is no upsell inside a consultation. If a remedy is relevant, the specification is given so that you may source it yourself from any vendor you trust. The consultation fee is the entire commercial relationship.

What if I do not know my exact birth time?

Mention it when booking. The ascendant moves roughly one degree every four minutes, so an uncertain birth time affects house placements. Where the time is uncertain, the reading is weighted toward the Moon chart and dasha periods, which are far more stable, and birth-time rectification can be discussed.

Can I book a consultation from outside India?

Yes. Sessions are held over video and clients in the United States, United Kingdom, United Arab Emirates, Singapore, Canada and Australia are regularly accommodated. Times outside standard Indian hours are arranged on request.

How is my information kept private?

Birth details, questions and session recordings are never shared, sold or published. Payment runs over a 256-bit SSL connection through Razorpay and no card or banking details are stored on our servers at any point.

What is the difference between the three tiers?

The Focused Session addresses one specific question in 40 minutes. The Full Chart Consultation covers the entire chart across 75 minutes with a written dossier and two weeks of follow-up. The Year Ahead adds a twelve-month dasha and transit timing map with two scheduled follow-up calls across the year.

What if the session does not address what I came for?

Write within seven days and a follow-up session is scheduled at no charge. Because each reading is prepared by hand for one person, completed digital readings are not refundable — but the follow-up commitment stands without exception.
