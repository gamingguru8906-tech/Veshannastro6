By Shashank Agrawal, founder of Veshannastro

The predictions for each person will depend on their Numerology Number or Moolank. This number can be calculated using your birth date. For example, people born on the 1st, 10th, 19th, or 28th fall under Numerology Number 1.

This guide provides a numerology forecast for all nine numerology numbers, covering career, money, business, love, relationships, personal growth and health.

2027 is the Moon's year (Universal Year 11/2). In the classical planetary friendships, the Moon supports the Sun, Jupiter and Mars, so Moolank 1, 3 and 9 find 2027 supportive. Its relationship with Rahu and Saturn is uneasy, so Moolank 4 and 8 need more patience. Moolank 2 sees the Moon doubled. Mercury, Venus and Ketu (Moolank 5, 6 and 7) see mixed results.

Numerology Number 1 (Sun - Supportive)

Career: A strong year for leadership and recognition. Promotions and new responsibilities tend to come through someone vouching for you rather than solo effort. Lead, but listen - the year's energy rewards leaders who build consensus.

Money: Steady growth rather than sudden gains. Income improves through position and reputation. Avoid ego-driven spending and hasty investments.

Business: Partnerships work well this year. Joint ventures, collaborations and client relationships bring growth. Sign agreements carefully and share credit.

Love and relationships: Warmth and commitment grow, but pride can cause friction. Say the appreciative thing out loud instead of assuming it is understood.

Personal growth and health: Confidence is high. Channel it into one clear goal rather than many. Keep a regular routine for sleep and exercise so energy stays steady.

Numerology Number 2 (Moon - Amplified)

Career: The Moon doubled means your sensitivity and people skills are your biggest strength. Support roles, client service, counselling, negotiation and teamwork are well placed. Progress comes through patience, not pressure.

Money: Joint finances, family money and savings plans do well. Avoid lending without paperwork and emotional spending.

Business: An excellent year for partnerships. Build alliances, fix contracts and strengthen client relationships. Do not go it alone where a good partner is available.

Love and relationships: Feelings run deep and small comments land harder than usual. This is one of the better years for engagement, marriage talks and repairing family bonds, provided difficult conversations are not avoided.

Personal growth and health: Mood swings are the main challenge. Fixed sleep times, enough water, walks and time away from screens matter more than usual. Do not make big decisions on a low day.

Numerology Number 3 (Jupiter - Supportive)

Career: Creativity, visibility and communication open doors. Teaching, writing, public-facing work and learning new skills are favoured. Your ideas get an audience this year.

Money: Growth comes through skills, networks and reputation. A good year for a raise or a better role, and for turning a talent into income.

Business: Expansion suits you - new offerings, collaborations and public visibility. Say yes to opportunities that put you in front of people, but finish what you start.

Love and relationships: Joyful and expressive. Social life expands, old friends reconnect, and romance benefits from honesty and humour. Avoid promising more than you can deliver.

Personal growth and health: Optimism is high, which is a strength and a risk - do not overcommit. Keep one main goal and protect your energy. Watch weight and restlessness.

Numerology Number 4 (Rahu - Needs care)

Career: A year of systems, patience and steady work rather than quick wins. Delays are likely; they are not failures. Paperwork, budgets and foundations laid now pay off later.

Money: Clear loans, fix budgets and avoid speculation. Money grows slowly and safely this year. Do not lend without paperwork or chase quick returns.

Business: Structure is everything - contracts, accounts, processes and reliable partners. Avoid risky expansion. Consolidate before growing.

Love and relationships: Rigidity can cause friction. Be flexible on small things and say what you feel plainly instead of withdrawing. Routine and reliability rebuild trust.

Personal growth and health: Stress shows up in sleep and digestion. A fixed routine, regular exercise and fewer late nights help more than big changes. Patience is the remedy.

Numerology Number 5 (Mercury - Mixed)

Career: Change is the theme - travel, job switches, new responsibilities and negotiations pick up. Adaptability is your advantage. Do not quit something good purely from restlessness.

Money: Money moves in and out quickly this year. Keep an emergency fund and avoid impulse trades. Gains come through movement and variety, not through holding still.

Business: New channels, new markets and new ways of working suit you. Test small before committing big. Flexibility beats rigid plans.

Love and relationships: New people enter your life and routine feels dull. Honest conversation keeps good relationships alive; boredom alone is not a reason to end one.

Personal growth and health: Nervous energy runs high. Ground yourself with regular exercise, time in nature and one stable daily habit. Avoid scattering attention across too many goals.

Numerology Number 6 (Venus - Pleasant, with friction)

Career: Client work, hospitality, beauty, design and all people-centred roles do well. Harmony at work brings recognition. Small frictions with colleagues pass quickly if handled kindly.

Money: Family money and comfort spending both rise. Enjoy life, but set a limit for luxuries so comfort does not become debt.

Business: Relationships are the business this year - repeat clients, referrals and warm service. Invest in how clients feel, not only in what you sell.

Love and relationships: One of the stronger years for commitment, marriage talks and home life. Give as much attention to your own needs as to others, or resentment builds quietly.

Personal growth and health: Balance is the lesson - home and self, giving and receiving. Rest, beauty and unhurried time at home restore you.

Numerology Number 7 (Ketu - Inward)

Career: Better for skills and qualifications than for switching jobs. Research, study, analysis and behind-the-scenes work are favoured. Recognition comes later than effort - keep going.

Money: Slow and steady. Avoid speculation and do not expect quick returns. Money spent on learning pays back best.

Business: Plan more than you launch. Research, refine and prepare; the second half of the year suits careful steps better than bold ones.

Love and relationships: You need space, and that is not rejection. Explain it kindly. Depth matters more than frequency - one honest conversation beats many shallow ones.

Personal growth and health: An inward year - reflection, spiritual practice, reading and quiet time restore you. Protect sleep and avoid overthinking by writing plans down.

Numerology Number 8 (Saturn - Demanding)

Career: Hard work is recognised, but slowly. Responsibility increases and authority grows through persistence. Do not expect shortcuts to work this year; steady effort does.

Money: Gains come through discipline - savings, structure and long-term plans. Clear old dues and avoid risky schemes. What is built carefully this year lasts.

Business: Build for the long term. Systems, reputation and reliable delivery matter more than fast growth. Avoid partnerships that depend on trust alone - put everything in writing.

Love and relationships: Duty can crowd out feeling. Make time for your partner and family deliberately, and say what you appreciate. Patience prevents small fights from becoming big ones.

Personal growth and health: Joints, back and fatigue need attention. Regular movement, oil massage, rest and a steady routine help. Do not carry everything alone - ask for support.

Numerology Number 9 (Mars - Supportive)

Career: Completion and leadership. Finishing projects, handing over cleanly and stepping into bigger responsibility all suit this year. Service-oriented work brings satisfaction.

Money: Gains come through closure - finishing, collecting and consolidating. Generosity returns to you, but give from surplus, not from savings.

Business: Finish what is pending before starting new ventures. Humanitarian and service angles strengthen your work. A good year to mentor and build goodwill.

Love and relationships: Passionate and direct. Old relationships either deepen or reach their natural end - both are healthy. Speak honestly and forgive quickly.

Personal growth and health: Energy is high; direct it, or it becomes impulsiveness. Exercise, early mornings and one clear cause keep you balanced. Avoid anger-driven decisions.

Remedies for 2027, a Moon year

These are the remedies Shashank sir suggests for a Moon year, and they suit everyone, whatever your Moolank:

- Offer water or milk to a Shivling on Mondays.
- Spend time with your mother or an elder woman in the family, and look after her needs.
- Keep a fixed sleep time and drink enough water.
- Wear white or silver on important days.
