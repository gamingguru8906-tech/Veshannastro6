The practice

Jyotish from Raipur, not from a call centre

Most people searching for an astrologer in Raipur end up on a national app — matched to whoever is free that evening, billed by the minute, and handed a software-generated PDF within the hour. The astrologer changes every time you call. Nobody remembers your chart.

Veshannastro is the practice of Shashank Agrawal, who lives and works in Raipur. He casts your chart himself before you meet, reads it with you over video, and writes your report by hand afterwards. There is one astrologer, and it is the same one every time.

The method is Parashari — the chart cast from precise birth data using the Lahiri ayanamsa, then read through house lords, the Navamsa and Dashamsha divisional charts, and the Vimshottari dasha sequence. Numerology, using the Lo Shu grid and compound analysis, is used to corroborate rather than to replace.

He is the author of Numbers & Navgraha and Untold Secret of Numerology, both published and available on Amazon — which means the method is on the record and open to scrutiny, not something you have to take on faith.

Questions

Asked by first-time clients in Raipur

Who is the best Vedic astrologer in Raipur?

Veshannastro is the practice of Shashank Agrawal, a Raipur-based Vedic astrologer and numerologist with over six years of one-to-one practice, more than 1,200 charts read, and two published books on numerology and Jyotish. Every reading is prepared by hand and conducted live — never generated by software. More usefully: judge any astrologer by whether they will show you their method, their credentials and a sample of their written work before you pay.

Do you offer consultations in Hindi?

Yes. Consultations are held in Hindi or English, whichever you are more comfortable with. Being based in Raipur also means sessions run on your own time zone, with no scheduling gymnastics.

Are consultations in person or online?

All consultations are held live over video. This keeps the full session recorded for you, and means the same reading is available whether you are in Raipur, elsewhere in Chhattisgarh, or abroad.

What areas of Chhattisgarh do you serve?

Clients across Raipur, Bhilai, Durg, Bilaspur, Korba, Rajnandgaon, Jagdalpur and Ambikapur, along with the rest of India and international clients in the United States, United Kingdom, United Arab Emirates, Singapore, Canada and Australia.

How much does a kundli reading in Raipur cost?

A Focused Session addressing one life area is ₹2,249 for 40 minutes. A Full Chart Consultation covering the entire chart is ₹4,999 for 75 minutes and includes a written dossier. Written reports without a live session start at ₹599. Every price is fixed in advance — there is no per-minute billing and no gemstone upsell afterwards.

What do I need before the session?

Your date of birth, time of birth as precisely as you can establish it, and place of birth — plus one to three questions you most want answered. If your birth time is uncertain, say so when booking: the reading will lean on the Moon chart and dasha periods, which are far more stable, and rectification can be discussed.
