Common questions

Should I include Pvt Ltd or LLP?

Only if people actually say it. Almost nobody includes the suffix in conversation, and numerology reads the name in use.

My business is already registered. Is it too late?

No. A spelling adjustment is usually far less disruptive than a full rename and can shift the vibration while keeping the name recognisable. The report covers that option.

Is numerology enough to choose a name?

No, and the report says so. Domain availability, trademark, pronunciation and longevity matter more. Use this to decide between finalists, not to generate them.
