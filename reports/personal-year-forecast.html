Common questions

Does the personal year run January to December?

It shifts around your birthday rather than the calendar year, which is why two people in the same calendar year can be in different personal years. The report makes your own dates explicit.

Is this a prediction of events?

No. It describes the character of a period — whether it favours beginnings, consolidation, or release. It does not claim to know what will happen to you, and any numerologist who says otherwise is overreaching.

Can I get this for a past year to check accuracy?

Yes, and it is a reasonable thing to ask for. Mention the year you want when you book and it will be prepared for that cycle instead.
