How it is prepared

The number is read digit by digit and as a total, then compared against your birth number. Compatibility is chart-specific — a number that suits one person can be poor for another, so no number is universally good or bad.

Every report on this site is written by a person. None are generated by software, and none are assembled from a template with your details substituted in. That is the reason they take hours rather than seconds.

Common questions

Do I include the country code?

No. The ten-digit subscriber number as you would dial it locally.

Is changing my number really worth it?

Often not. Changing a number means updating banks, clients, two-factor codes and years of contacts, and the numerological gain is usually modest. The report will tell you honestly if your current number is fine.

Can I have a number checked before I buy it?

Yes — that is the best time to ask. Send the numbers you are choosing between and they will be compared against your chart.
