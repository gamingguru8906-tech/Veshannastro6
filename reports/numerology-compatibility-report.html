Common questions

What if I do not know my partner's exact birth date?

The date is essential for this report. The name alone gives a much thinner reading, and it would not be honest to sell it as compatibility analysis.

Does a low compatibility score mean we should not be together?

No, and the report does not frame it that way. Numbers describe where friction is likely, not whether a relationship should exist. Plenty of durable relationships have difficult number combinations and manage them consciously.

Can this be done without my partner knowing?

Technically yes, since it only needs a name and date. Whether that is a good idea is a separate question, and the report will not tell you anything that substitutes for talking to them.
