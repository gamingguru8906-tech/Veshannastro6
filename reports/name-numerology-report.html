Common questions

Should I give my legal name or the name people call me?

The one people call you. Numerology reads the name in use — if your certificate says one thing and everyone says another, the spoken name is the operating vibration. Mention both when booking if they differ.

Will a name correction actually change my life?

It changes the vibration you project, and only once the new spelling is genuinely in use — signature, documents, how people address you. It is not a substitute for competence, timing or effort, and the report says so.

Is adding an extra letter enough?

Often, yes. A doubled letter or a changed vowel can shift a total without making the name unrecognisable, which matters because a name nobody can spell creates more friction than it removes.
