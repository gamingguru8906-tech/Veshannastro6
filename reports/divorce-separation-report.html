Common questions

Will this tell me whether to leave?

No. That decision is yours and nobody else's, and a chart is not entitled to make it for you. The report describes patterns and timing so you have more information than you had before.

What if I do not have my partner's birth details?

Provide whatever you have and write unknown for the rest. The report is prepared around what is available; missing partner details make the compatibility section thinner but the rest still stands.

Is this confidential?

Yes. Birth details, the report and anything you share are never disclosed to anyone. This applies to every reading here, and particularly to this one.

Before you order this one

If you are in immediate distress, a written report is not the right first step and it would be wrong to suggest otherwise. Please speak to someone you trust, or to a qualified counsellor.
