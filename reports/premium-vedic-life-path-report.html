Common questions

What if I do not know my exact birth time?

Say so when you book. Birth time materially affects the Lagna and therefore much of the report. Where the time is uncertain, rectification can be discussed, or a different report may suit you better.

How is this different from the numerology reports?

Entirely different system. This is Vedic astrology from your birth chart — planetary positions, houses and dasha periods. Numerology works from your date of birth and name only, and needs no birth time.

Will this predict specific events with dates?

It gives timing windows, not dated events. Vedic analysis can be specific about periods of pressure or opportunity; it does not claim to name what will happen on a given day, and this report will not pretend otherwise.
