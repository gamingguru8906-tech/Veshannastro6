Not sure which one?

If you want the broadest picture from numerology, start with the Complete Numerology Report. If you want your actual birth chart read rather than your numbers, the Premium Vedic Life Path Report is the equivalent on the astrology side.

For a single specific question — a name, a phone number, a business name — the individual reports are cheaper and answer that question properly. You can also try the free calculators first to see the raw numbers before deciding whether you want them interpreted.

Delivery and refunds

Most reports arrive within 2–3 hours; a few take up to 24–48 hours where they involve name generation or full chart calculation. Each product page states its own window.
