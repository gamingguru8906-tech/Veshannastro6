Common questions

How do I photograph my palms properly?

Natural daylight, palm flat and fully open, whole hand in frame, camera steady and close enough that the fine lines are visible. Both hands, photographed separately. Poor photos produce a poor reading.

Why do you need both hands?

The non-dominant hand is traditionally read as what you were born with; the dominant hand as what you have made of it. The difference between them is often the most interesting part.

Do I need my birth details?

Not for the palmistry itself, though they are collected at booking so the report can be matched to you.
