How it is prepared

Names are selected against your child's numbers rather than picked from a ranked list. If you have preferences — a starting letter, a family sound, a particular tradition — those constrain the search rather than being ignored.

Every report on this site is written by a person. None are generated by software, and none are assembled from a template with your details substituted in. That is the reason they take hours rather than seconds.

Common questions

Can I ask for names starting with a specific letter?

Yes. Many families have a preferred initial from a family tradition or an astrological suggestion. Give it when booking and the suggestions will work within it.

What if my child is not born yet?

The report needs a date of birth, so it is best prepared after the birth. If you want to prepare in advance, options can be worked out for the expected date and adjusted once the actual date is known.

Do you consider the meaning of the name too?

Yes. A name that is numerologically ideal but means something unfortunate is not a good recommendation, so meaning and sound are both taken into account.
