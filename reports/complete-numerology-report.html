Common questions

How is this different from the free calculators on this site?

The calculators give you one number at a time with a general meaning. This report reads all of your numbers against each other, which is where the actual insight is — a Life Path 7 with a Destiny 3 means something quite different from a 7 with a Destiny 7.

Do I need my birth time?

No. Numerology works from your date of birth and your name. Birth time matters for Vedic astrology reports, not for this one.

Will you tell me to change my name?

Only if the analysis genuinely supports it, and the report explains the reasoning either way. A name correction is a real practice but it is also the most oversold thing in numerology, and it will not be recommended for its own sake.
