How to calculate your personal year number for 2027

Your Personal Year number describes the theme of one calendar year for you. It changes every year and moves through a cycle from 1 to 9. Three numbers go into it: your birth day, your birth month and the year itself.

  1. Reduce your birth day to one digit. Born on the 23rd: 2 + 3 = 5. Born on the 8th: it stays 8.
  2. Reduce your birth month to one digit. January is 1, September is 9, October is 1 + 0 = 1, November is 1 + 1 = 2, December is 1 + 2 = 3.
  3. Reduce the year. 2027 is 2 + 0 + 2 + 7 = 11, and 1 + 1 = 2.
  4. Add the three and reduce to one digit. Born 23 November: 5 + 2 + 2 = 9. Personal Year 9.

Your birth year is not part of this. It is used for your life path number, which stays the same all your life.

When the sum passes through 11

If your birth day and month add up with 2 to exactly 11 before you reduce, as for someone born on 4 May (4 + 5 + 2 = 11), many numerologists call it an 11/2 Personal Year: a Personal Year 2 with the master number 11's sharper intuition and nervous energy. Personal Months inside the year

Each month also has a number: your Personal Year plus the calendar month, reduced. If you are in Personal Year 3, March is 3 + 3 = 6 and October is 3 + 10 = 13, which reduces to 4. Each Personal Year post lists all twelve months for you.

Personal Year, Mulank and life path: the difference

Personal Year: birth day + birth month + current year. Changes every year. Describes the year's theme.

Mulank (birth number): your birth day reduced. Never changes. Describes your nature and how a year's ruling planet sits with yours. See Mulank predictions 2027.

Life path number (Bhagyank): your full date of birth reduced. Never changes. Describes your life's direction. Calculate it with the life path number calculator.

Questions

How do I calculate my personal year number for 2027?

Reduce your birth day to one digit, reduce your birth month to one digit, add 2 for 2027, and reduce the total to one digit. Born 14 March: 1 + 4 = 5, March is 3, so 5 + 3 + 2 = 10, and 1 + 0 = 1. Personal Year 1.

Do I use my birth year?

No. The Personal Year uses your birth day, your birth month and the current year. Your birth year is used for your life path number (Bhagyank), which does not change.

Why is 2027 counted as 2?

Because 2 + 0 + 2 + 7 = 11, and 1 + 1 = 2. Some numerologists keep the 11 visible and call 2027 an 11/2 year.

When does my Personal Year start: 1 January or my birthday?

This calculator uses the calendar year, 1 January to 31 December, which is the most common method. Some numerologists start it on your birthday instead; if you follow that method, read the year before as well for the months before your birthday.

Yes. It runs in your browser, needs no sign-up, and nothing you type is stored.
