How these predictions are read

2027 adds up to 11, which reduces to 2, and 2 is the Moon's number. Each Mulank is ruled by a planet: 1 Sun, 2 Moon, 3 Jupiter, 4 Rahu, 5 Mercury, 6 Venus, 7 Ketu, 8 Saturn, 9 Mars. The classical friendships between planets set the tone: the Sun, Jupiter and Mars are friends of the Moon; Rahu and Saturn are not. That gives each Mulank its starting point for the year.

Supportive. The Sun and the Moon are natural friends, so 2027 works with Mulank 1 rather than against it. Leadership comes more easily when it is paired with listening. Recognition through seniors, government contacts and established institutions is likely. Watch ego in partnerships: the Moon year rewards warmth more than command.

Questions

What is Mulank?

Mulank, also spelled moolank, is your birth number in Indian numerology: the day you were born, reduced to one digit. Born on the 27th: 2 + 7 = 9, so Mulank 9.

How is Bhagyank calculated?

Add every digit of your full date of birth and reduce to one digit. Born 27/09/1994: 2 + 7 + 0 + 9 + 1 + 9 + 9 + 4 = 41, and 4 + 1 = 5. Bhagyank 5.

Which Mulank is luckiest in 2027?

In a Moon year, Mulank 1, 3 and 9 have the most natural support, and Mulank 2 has the year's energy doubled. Mulank 4 and 8 need more patience. Every Mulank has good months; they show up in your Personal Year.

2027 kaisa rahega mulank ke anusar?

2027 ka ank 2 hai, jiska swami Chandrama hai. Mulank 1, 3 aur 9 ke liye saal sahayak hai, Mulank 2 ke liye Chandra ki urja dugni hai, Mulank 5, 6 aur 7 ke liye mila-jula hai, aur Mulank 4 aur 8 ke liye dhairya ka saal hai.

What is the difference between Mulank and Personal Year?

Mulank describes your nature and never changes. Your Personal Year uses your birth day and birth month with the current year, so it changes every year. Read both: Mulank for how the year sits with you, Personal Year for the year's theme.
